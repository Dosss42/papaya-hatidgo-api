Step 1

Install the app

Papaya HatidGo isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it from this page. It takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APK. Scan the QR code above, or tap Download the app (APK) on your phone.
  2. Open the file. When the download finishes, tap it in the notification, or find papaya-hatidgo.apk in your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install. If Android says the browser can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognize the app, tap More details › Install anyway. That warning appears for every app that isn't from the Play Store.
  5. Open Papaya HatidGo. Its tricycle icon is now on your home screen.

iThe app needs the internet to work. It asks for your location (so drivers can find you) and for notifications (so you hear about your ride when the app is closed).

Step 2

Getting started

Choose your language, then create an account as a passenger or a driver. One account is one role.

Choose your language

On the first screen, tap English or Taglish (Tagalog with English words). You can change it later in Account › Language.

Create an account

  1. Tap Get Started.
  2. Under Who are you?, choose Passenger or Driver.
  3. Enter your first and last name, mobile number, email and a password (8 or more characters, with a letter and a number).
  4. Tap Create account. You're logged in right away.

Log in

Tap I already have an account, then enter your email or mobile number and your password.

Forgot your password?

  1. On Log in, tap Forgot password?
  2. Enter your account's email and tap Send code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code from your email, choose a new password, and tap Change password.
For passengers

Booking a tricycle

The app has four tabs at the bottom: Book, Rides, Alerts and Account. You pay the driver in cash when the ride ends.

First, subscribe

Booking needs an active subscription. Tap Subscribe on the Book tab, or go to Account › Subscription.

  1. Choose a plan: 1 month, 6 months or 12 months. The longer plans include free months. The admin sets the prices.
  2. Tap Pay. You pay on PayMongo's secure page with GCash, Maya or a card. Papaya HatidGo never sees your card or PIN.
  3. Come back to the app. It shows Payment received! and the date your subscription runs until.

iWhile the project is in test mode, no real money is charged. You can Renew early at any time, and the new months start when the current ones end, so no paid day is lost.

Book in three steps

  1. Pickup. The map starts at your location. Move the map so the pin sits where the driver should pick you up, or tap Use my location. You can also search a place, such as the public market or a school. The app names the spot for you, and you can add a landmark (for example blue gate). Tap Pick me up here.
  2. Destination. Move the map to where you're going, or search it. Tap I'm going here.
  3. Choose your ride. The fare for each ride type is shown. Pick one, then tap Book · ₱….

iRides are only inside the service area (Papaya). The fare is measured along the real roads, not in a straight line.

One-way or Balikan?

One-way

Drop-off only. The driver takes you to your destination and the ride ends there.

Balikan

The driver waits for you, then brings you back to the pickup. Choose how long you'll stay: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, or Not sure yet.

Tap See details to see how the fare is made:

  • Base fare plus the distance (km × the rate per km). Short trips cost at least the minimum fare.
  • Balikan adds the way back, and a waiting fee if the admin has set one. The waiting rule is shown, for example First 10 min free, then ₱2/min. You're charged for the time you really wait.
  • A service fee appears only if the admin has set one.

The fare shown is an estimate. You can add a message for the driver (for example I have a big bag) before booking.

During the ride

After booking, the ride screen shows each stage with a progress bar:

  1. Looking for a driver… Nearby drivers see your request. If nobody accepts in time, the app says No driver is available right now, and your booking is kept so you can try again.
  2. [Driver] is on the way. You see the driver's name, rating, tricycle color, body number and plate number, and how far away they are. Tap the phone button to call them.
  3. [Driver] is here. Look for the tricycle with that body number.
  4. You're on the way to your destination.

Cancelling

You can cancel while the app is looking for a driver, and after a driver accepts. Once the driver taps I'm on my way, you can't cancel right away. The app shows the time when you may cancel (10 minutes later, set by the admin) in case the driver hasn't arrived.

Balikan: waiting and coming back

At your destination, the driver waits. The screen shows how long you said you'd stay and how many minutes have passed. When you're ready to go home, tap I'm coming back so the driver gets ready.

Paying and rating

When the ride ends, the app shows Pay [driver] ₱… (cash). If the final fare is different from the estimate (for example, you waited less), both are shown. Then rate the ride from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional note, or tap Later.

Rides and Alerts

Rides lists your rides, newest first, with the fare and status. You can rate a ride you skipped there. Alerts collects ride updates, payment news and reminders.

Your account

The Account tab shows your name and number, the language, your subscription, and Logout.

For tricycle drivers

Driving with Papaya HatidGo

The driver app has five tabs: Home, Rides, Earnings, Alerts and Account. Before your first passenger, the admin checks your papers.

Get verified (one time)

Home shows a checklist. Finish it in order:

  1. Your tricycle. Enter the plate number and color (body number, brand and model are optional). Tap Add tricycle.
  2. Documents. Upload a clear photo (or PDF) of each: Driver's license, Tricycle's OR/CR, Franchise / MTOP permit and Clearance, with the expiry date. Make sure all four corners show, the text is clear, and there's no flash glare.
  3. Wait for the admin. Each document shows Under review, then Approved or Needs fixing with the admin's reason. Fix it and upload again.
  4. Subscribe. Once you're verified, choose a plan and pay, just like passengers.

iOnly the Papaya HatidGo admin can see your documents. When a document is close to expiring, the app reminds you. Upload the new copy early, and the old one keeps counting until it's approved.

Go online and choose passengers

  1. On Home, tap Go online and allow your location.
  2. Requests near you lists every open booking in town, nearest first: the pickup, how far it is from you, the trip length, the fare, and One-way or Balikan.
  3. Tap a request to see the details (and the passenger's message), then tap Accept or Skip. If another driver took it first, the app tells you.

Auto-accept

Tap Auto-accept and choose a distance from 2 to 5 km. A request whose pickup is that close is accepted for you, and the ride opens. Choose Off: I choose myself to stop it.

iWhile you're online, the screen stays on and a sound plays for new requests, so turn the volume up and keep the phone charged. Tap Go offline when you stop driving.

The ride, one button at a time

The big button at the bottom always shows the next step. Tap it as each one happens:

Use Directions to open Google Maps to the pickup or drop-off, and the phone button to call the passenger.

Balikan waiting

The screen shows how long the passenger said they'd stay and the minutes so far. When they tap I'm coming back, you see [Passenger] is coming back! If they never return, tap The passenger didn't come back: they pay for the trip there and the waiting, not the way back.

Cancelling

Tap Cancel and choose a reason: tricycle problem, can't find the passenger, the passenger didn't show up, or another reason.

Earnings

The Earnings tab adds up the cash from your finished rides for Today, This week and This month, with the list of rides. Home also shows today's total.

Taken offline?

The app takes you offline if your subscription ends, a document expires or needs fixing, your account is suspended, or your location stops arriving for a while. Home tells you why and links to the fix.

For the administrator

The admin website

Papaya HatidGo is run from a separate website on a computer, for the admin only. Admin accounts can't log in to the phone app.

Dashboard

Today's rides, fares, drivers online and active subscriptions.

Users and verification

Review each driver's documents and tricycle, then approve, reject with a reason, or ask for a fix. Suspend or reactivate accounts.

Rides and live map

See online drivers and active rides on a map, along the roads they take.

Fares and settings

Set the base fare, rate per km, minimum fare, Balikan way back and waiting fee, and the service fee. Switch ride types on or off, and set the service area and the matching rules.

Plans, reports and audit log

Edit subscription plans, read ride and income reports, and see who changed what.

Help

Questions and fixes

The phone won't install the APK.

Allow your browser (or Files app) to install unknown apps: phone Settings › Apps › [your browser] › Install unknown apps. If an older Papaya HatidGo is installed and the install fails, uninstall it first, then install again.

The app says "No internet, or the server can't be reached."

Check that mobile data or Wi-Fi is on, then tap Try again. If it still fails, the Papaya HatidGo server may be offline for a moment.

The map doesn't find my location.

Turn on GPS (Location) and allow it for Papaya HatidGo in Settings › Apps › Papaya HatidGo › Permissions. You can always drag the map to the right spot instead.

It says "We're not in this area yet."

Rides are only inside the Papaya service area. Move the pin to a place inside it.

I don't hear about my ride when the app is closed.

Turn on notifications: phone Settings › Apps › Papaya HatidGo › Notifications. Drivers should also keep the volume up to hear new requests.

Why can't I cancel after the driver is on the way?

So drivers don't waste a trip. If the driver still hasn't arrived after the time shown on your ride screen, the Cancel button comes back.

Why is the final fare different from the estimate?

For Balikan, the waiting fee uses the time you really waited, and if you didn't come back, there's no way-back fare. A ride always keeps the fare rules from when it was booked, even if the admin changes the prices later.

As a driver, I don't see any requests.

Make sure you're online, your location is on, and your documents, tricycle and subscription are all approved and active. Requests only appear while someone is booking.
